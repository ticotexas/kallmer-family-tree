# 06 --- Version 2 Roadmap

## Purpose

Version 1 established the Kallmer Family Archive as a polished public
genealogy archive.

Version 2 expands that foundation into a digital historical
archive---one that seeks not only to answer **who** the family is, but
**how the family became what it is**.

This document describes the archive's long-term direction. It
intentionally focuses on enduring goals rather than implementation order
or individual coding tasks.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Guiding Vision

The Kallmer Family Archive should continue evolving as a thoughtfully
curated digital local-history museum centered on one family.

Every future enhancement should deepen historical understanding while
preserving the archive's visual restraint, maintainable architecture,
and editorial quality.

Version 2 emphasizes interpretation, context, and exploration rather
than simply adding features.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Four Lenses

## People

Continue expanding each individual profile into a richer historical
exhibit.

Long-term goals include:

-   richer biographies;
-   expanded family stories;
-   historical documents;
-   source citations;
-   personal timelines;
-   improved portrait presentation;
-   additional photograph metadata;
-   contextual historical interpretation.

## Relationships

Continue strengthening how families are understood across generations.

Potential future work includes:

-   expanded relationship narratives;
-   clearer marriage chronology;
-   richer family histories;
-   adoption, foster, and step-family presentation where documented;
-   improved family-unit visualization;
-   continued usability refinements for the Interactive Family Tree.

## Places

Places become a first-class method of exploring the archive.

Long-term goals include:

-   place-centered browsing;
-   migration paths derived from documented events;
-   historically appropriate maps;
-   geographic context for photographs, stories, and documents;
-   exploration by community, county, state, region, and country;
-   historical place descriptions where appropriate.

The Places experience should resemble a historical atlas rather than a
modern mapping application.

## Time

Chronology becomes another way to experience the archive.

Future possibilities include:

-   individual timelines;
-   family timelines;
-   generation views;
-   historical events alongside family history;
-   chronological browsing of stories, photographs, and documents.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Homepage

The homepage remains the museum lobby.

Future enhancements may include:

-   Featured Person;
-   Featured Story;
-   Featured Photograph;
-   Featured Historical Document;
-   rotating exhibits drawn from the archive;
-   seasonal or anniversary features;
-   improved pathways into the collection.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Interactive Family Tree

The Interactive Family Tree remains the archive's primary finding aid.

Future work should continue refining:

-   composition;
-   spacing;
-   responsiveness;
-   accessibility;
-   navigation;
-   large-family presentation;
-   subtle orientation animations;
-   keyboard support where appropriate.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Historical Documents

Version 2 should increasingly integrate historical source material,
including census records, military records, newspapers, cemetery
records, land records, letters, certificates, and family papers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Archive Infrastructure

Continue strengthening shared infrastructure through incremental
improvement.

Long-term goals include:

-   reusable archive components;
-   continued documentation refinement;
-   maintainable architecture;
-   improved data generation;
-   privacy-aware public and private archive support;
-   eventual authenticated private archive for family members.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Editorial Direction

Future work should continue improving:

-   historical accuracy;
-   writing quality;
-   narrative structure;
-   source preservation;
-   contextual interpretation;
-   consistency across biographies and stories.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Guiding Principles

Version 2 should continue valuing:

-   historical authenticity over visual novelty;
-   curation over feature accumulation;
-   understanding over complexity;
-   restraint over decoration;
-   maintainability over unnecessary redesign.

Every new capability should strengthen the archive's identity as a
digital historical museum.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Looking Beyond Version 2

The archive should remain capable of growing for many years while
preserving its central purpose:

To document, understand, preserve, and share the history of the Kallmer
family through thoughtfully curated people, relationships, places, and
time.
