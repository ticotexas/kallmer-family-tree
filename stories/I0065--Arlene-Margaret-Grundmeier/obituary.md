## Arlene M. (Bernhardt) Grundmeier

**May 3, 1922 – August 6, 2017**

## Early Life & Education

Arlene Margaret Grundmeier, 95, of Storm Lake, Iowa, passed away on August 6, 2017, at Methodist Manor Retirement Community.

She was born on May 3, 1922, on a farm south of Early, Iowa, the daughter of **Fred Bernhardt** and **Isabell (Kallmer) Bernhardt**. She spent her early years north of Odebolt before her family moved to the Schaller area, where she graduated from Schaller High School in 1939.

After high school, Arlene attended Buena Vista College, earning a two-year teaching certificate. She taught school in Galva, Iowa, for two years before beginning the next chapter of her life.

## Marriage & Family

On July 10, 1944, Arlene married **Art Grundmeier** at St. Joseph Catholic Church in Schaller. Together they built a life on their farm in Eden Township, where they raised four children—Ann, Barbara, Carol, and Dennis—and remained for fifty-one years.

In 1995, Arlene and Art moved to Emerald Park in Storm Lake. They later made their home at Lake Point Villa.

## Faith, Service & Community

Faith and service were central to Arlene's life. She was an active member of St. Joseph Catholic Church in Schaller, serving in the Rosary Society, the Catholic Daughters of America, and the Legion of Mary, holding numerous leadership positions through the years.

She also devoted herself to her community through the Buena Vista Hospital Auxiliary, Sac County Hospice, and a variety of local social organizations. For fifty-eight years she enjoyed fellowship through the Pinochle Couple Club.

Arlene especially loved traveling and found great joy in attending the activities and accomplishments of her grandchildren.
