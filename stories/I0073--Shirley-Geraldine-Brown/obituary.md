## Notice of Death

Shirley Geraldine Brown, 91, of Tripoli, Iowa and formerly of Waterloo, Iowa, died on Wednesday, October 6, 2010, at the Tripoli Nursing Home.

## Early Life & Education

Shirley was born on May 2, 1919, in Postville, Iowa, the daughter of Walter and Tilda (Olson) Huffey. She was baptized and confirmed at Old East Paint Creek Church in Waterville, Iowa. She graduated from Decorah High School.

## Marriage & Career

Shirley was united in marriage to LuVerne Brown on August 12, 1940, in Burr Oak, Iowa. She worked at Sears and Roebuck in Waterloo, retiring in 1982.

## Faith & Hobbies

She was a member of Grace Lutheran Church in Waterloo. Shirley enjoyed fishing, traveling, painting with oils, restoring furniture, bowling, crocheting, cooking, playing Scrabble, and crossword puzzles. She enjoyed all types of music, especially bluegrass, but most of all Shirley enjoyed spending time with her family.

## Surviving Family

Shirley is survived by her husband, LuVerne of Tripoli; three daughters, Jacquelyn (Herb) Clemen of Readlyn, Iowa, Janet Hagen of Anderson, South Carolina, and Jeanne Downer of Gladstone, Missouri; one son, Lyndon Brown of Kansas City, Missouri; two daughters-in-law, Judith Brown of Aurora, Illinois, and Benita Brown of Waterloo, Iowa; 25 grandchildren, 37 great-grandchildren, and 14 great-great-grandchildren; one brother, George (Jean) Huffey of Waterville, Iowa; and four sisters, Greta Lockhart of Brownsville, Minnesota, Leatrice McEvilly of Caledonia, Minnesota, Esther Enyart of Texas, and Lotus (Barton) Bulman of Caledonia, Minnesota.

## Preceded in Death

She was preceded in death by her parents; three brothers, Lloyd Huffey, Vinton Huffey, and Vernon Huffey; one sister, Merle Schultz; three children, Ronald, Sharon Paulsen, and Richard; one son-in-law, Bob Hagen; and four grandsons, Brian Brown, Derek Clemen, Eric Rule, and Andrew Harris.

## Services & Memorials

Funeral services were held on Saturday, October 9, 2010, at 10:00 a.m. at St. Paul's Lutheran Church in Readlyn. Burial was in Waterloo Memorial Park Cemetery, Waterloo. Memorials were directed to Cedar Valley Hospice, Waverly, or the Tripoli Nursing Home.
