Earlier, my dad had given us a small RV trailer, and for several weeks I
lived in it near 84th and F Streets. Everyone else stayed with LaDon's
parents in Storm Lake.

In a couple of months, and with the help of a realtor, we purchased a
split-level house at 14824 Drexel Street in the Stonybrook subdivision.

I worked for HDR for 28 years as the manager and lead site designer on
many architectural projects throughout the United States.

LaDon and I became avid collectors of Heisey glass. My stepmother,
Dorcas, would give us Heisey as gifts, and that started the disease.

In 2000, we inherited a 1978 Airstream trailer from her parents. In
2006, we purchased a 31-foot 2003 Airstream and a 2002 Ford Excursion.
We put 204,000 miles on the Excursion in those 20 years, or 10,200 miles
per year. I was president of the local Nebraska Airstream Club in 2006
and again in 2014.

In 2004, I retired at age 62, and LaDon also retired the same year as a
medical secretary.
