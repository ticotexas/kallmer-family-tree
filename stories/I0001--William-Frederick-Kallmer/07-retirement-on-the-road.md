We spent the following 26 years traveling with other friends to camping
spots throughout the United States.

From 2004 to 2006, we pulled the Airstream and spent winters in Texas,
where Tim and his wife, Sunny, were living in Austin.

In 2008, our daughter Cherlyn and her husband, Brent, moved from Omaha
to Acworth, Georgia. In December 2008, Cherlyn and Brent had a baby boy,
Reed, and later, in September 2011, they had twins, Brielle and Cole.
That changed some of our travel plans.

We would first drive south to Texas and stay there a few days, travel
east to Acworth, then drive south to an RV site in North Fort Myers.

In 2020, Tim and Sunny purchased 38 acres in the countryside of
Sweetwater, Tennessee. Now, traveling to Florida, we stop first in
Sweetwater for a few days, then drive to Acworth for more family time.

In July 2022, we purchased a double-wide home in Southwind RV and Mobile
Home Park in North Fort Myers. We no longer pull the Airstream behind
the Excursion.

On September 28, 2022, Hurricane Ian hit the Fort Myers area, and many
homes in our park were tragically damaged. Our home was spared with very
little damage, and we were grateful for that.

In the first part of April or May, we reverse our travels and head back
to Omaha.
