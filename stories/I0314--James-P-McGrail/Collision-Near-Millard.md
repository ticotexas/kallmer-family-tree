On the evening of September 8, 1932, James McGrail and his wife, Helen (Urban) McGrail, were traveling near Millard, Nebraska, when their automobile collided head-on with a transfer truck about seven miles southwest of town.

Helen died while being transported to Omaha for medical treatment. James was taken to Lord Lister Hospital in critical condition with multiple fractures and internal injuries. Although he survived the crash and was able to escape the burning wreckage, he died the following afternoon.

James operated a cigar store at Fourteenth and M Streets in Lincoln.

Investigators later determined that the truck driver was not at fault. Witnesses testified that the truck was traveling within the governor-limited speed for commercial vehicles, while James's automobile crossed the center line and struck the truck head-on.

The deaths of James and Helen deeply affected both families. Only weeks later, James's father, John J. McGrail, died. Relatives believed the grief following the loss of his son and daughter-in-law may have hastened his death.
