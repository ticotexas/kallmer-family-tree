Following the fatal automobile collision that claimed the lives of James and Helen (Urban) McGrail, another mystery emerged.

James was reported to have been carrying approximately $280 in cash from his cigar store. After the wreck, neither hospital staff nor investigating officers could locate the money. Police questioned everyone known to have been at the scene.

Witnesses testified that James carried his money in a leather satchel strapped to his arm rather than in a wallet. One rescuer recalled helping move James away from the burning automobile but did not remember seeing the satchel afterward.

Investigators also considered whether the cash had been destroyed in the fire that consumed the automobile. Fragments of Helen's purse were found among the burned debris, but the money was never recovered.

An inquest concluded that the truck driver bore no responsibility for the collision, and the disappearance of the money remained unexplained.
