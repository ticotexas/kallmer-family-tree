Anna Elizabeth (Smidt) Urban was born on December 3, 1872, in Hamelburg, Germany. As a child, she immigrated with her parents to the Millard area of Nebraska.

## Marriage and Family

In 1890, Anna married John C. Urban, and together they raised nine children. Four of their children preceded her in death: Herman, Chris, August, and Helen.

After living on a farm near Ashland, the family moved into Ashland, where Anna lived for 37 years. Following the death of her husband in September 1928, she continued to make her home in Ashland before later residing with A. B. Miller until her death.

## Faith

Anna was a longtime member of the Congregational Church in Ashland.

## Final Years

In declining health for several years, Anna died on March 18, 1958, at Immanuel Hospital in Omaha after a week-long illness. She was 85 years old.

## Family

She was survived by two sons, John of Hooper and Walter of Wahoo; three daughters, Martha (Mrs. Smith) of Burbank, California, Ella (Mrs. O. A. Hemmer) of Omaha, and Minna Hood of Wahoo; three sisters, Ida Daemon of Omaha, Mathilda Dixon of Sacramento, California, and Mathilda Mason of Lincoln; seven grandchildren; six great-grandchildren; and numerous nieces and nephews.

## Burial

Funeral services were held at the First Congregational Church in Ashland on March 20, 1958. Burial followed at Ashland Cemetery.
