Among the family papers preserved by Craig Lease is a handwritten account of the life of Ellen Steele. Craig believes it may have been written by either Ellen's daughter Helga Steele Hanson Medin or Helga's daughter Lillian Hanson.

The paper records that Mrs. Ellen Steele was born August 1, 1867, in Sweden, in a place Craig transcribed from the difficult handwriting as "Hybugdin Sweden Vestra Torsis."

She married Swen M. Steele on December 29, 1894. Seven children were born to the couple. Two died in Sweden, while five survived:

- Oscar A. Steele, of Rockford, Illinois
- Mrs. Anton Hanson, of Cherokee, Iowa
- Mrs. Thure Larson, of San Diego, California
- Selma Steele, of Chicago, Illinois
- Mrs. Floyd Kallmer, of Storm Lake, Iowa

The handwritten account gives Ellen's parents as Jens Lersson and Ingrid Perssan, although Craig noted that some of the handwriting was difficult to read and these spellings may not be correct.

According to the paper, Swen Steele emigrated to America in April 1908 and settled in Alta, Iowa. Ellen and their five children followed in the latter part of June 1909. The family thereafter lived in Iowa, except for a few years spent on the Anton Hanson farm south of Cherokee.

Ellen was a member of Trinity Lutheran Church in Alta and participated in several church societies, devoting much of her time to them when she was able to attend.

At the time the account was written, Ellen had eight grandchildren and five great-grandchildren. The grandchildren identified on the paper were:

- Edith's sons: Bob, Jim, and Bill
- Nellie's son: Ellsworth
- Helga's children: Bill, Lillian, Helen, and Glen

*Source: Handwritten family paper transcribed by Craig Lease, son of Helen and grandson of Helga Steele Hanson Medin, Northwood, Iowa, August 27, 2026. Craig noted that some words and names were difficult to decipher and his transcription may contain errors.*
