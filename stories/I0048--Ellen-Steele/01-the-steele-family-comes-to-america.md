In June 1909, Elin Steele and her five children began their journey from Europe to a new life in America.

The family left from Liverpool on June 25, 1909, aboard the *Cedric*. Elin was 41 years old. Traveling with her were:

- Oscar, 14
- Helga, 11
- Nellie, 9
- Selma, 5
- Edith, 1

Their destination was the United States, where they arrived through Ellis Island.

A separate handwritten family account says that Elin's husband, Swen M. Steele, had emigrated to America in April 1908 and settled in Alta, Iowa. Elin and the five children followed him the next year, beginning the American chapter of the Steele family.

*Source: Family information supplied by Craig Lease, grandson of Helga Steele Hanson Medin, August 27, 2026.*
