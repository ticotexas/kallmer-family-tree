## Early Life & Education

Helen Margurite Lease was born on May 21, 1920, in Silver Township, Cherokee County, Iowa, the daughter of Anton and Helga (Steele) Hanson. She was baptized on July 21, 1920, at Bethlehem Lutheran Church in Cherokee and confirmed on June 12, 1955, at Grace Lutheran Church in Storm Lake. Helen graduated from Wilson High School in Cherokee in 1938. Following graduation, she worked at Jantzen's Department Store in Storm Lake.

## Marriage & Family

Helen was united in marriage to Al Lease on June 23, 1940, at the Presbyterian Parsonage in Cherokee. The couple lived in Cherokee until Al entered military service. Following his return, they moved to Storm Lake in 1946.

## Community & Later Life

Helen was a longtime member of Grace Lutheran Church and dedicated her life to helping others. She will be remembered for her faithful devotion to her family as a wife, mother, and homemaker.
