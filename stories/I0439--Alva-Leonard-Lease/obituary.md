## Early Life & Education

Alva Leonard Lease was born on January 14, 1918, on a farm near Lawton, Iowa, the son of Leonard and Edith (Finna) Lease. He moved to Cherokee at a young age and received his education in the public schools, graduating from Wilson High School in Cherokee in 1938.

## Marriage & Military Service

Al was united in marriage to Helen Margurite Hanson on June 23, 1940, at the Presbyterian Parsonage in Cherokee. He was employed at Knipes Shoe Store for four years before entering military service on December 21, 1941. He served in the United States Army Air Forces from 1942 to 1944 as a member of the Eighth Air Force, 305th Bomb Group, and 365th Bomb Squadron, based at Chelveston, England. He served as a flight engineer and top-turret gunner aboard a B-17, completing 34 combat missions over Europe.

## Career & Later Life

Upon returning from the war, Al and Helen moved to Storm Lake in 1946, where Al was employed at Kallmer Sales until 1966. After the business was sold, he joined Merrill Manufacturing Company, where he worked from 1967 until retiring in 1973. Al and Helen moved to Northwood in July 2001 to be closer to their children.

## Community & Character

Al was a longtime member of Grace Lutheran Church in Storm Lake. He will be remembered as a man of high integrity who served as a wonderful role model as a husband and father.

## Family Precedents

He was preceded in death by his parents; his wife, Helen, on October 17, 2001; his brother, Glen; and his nephew, Stephen.
