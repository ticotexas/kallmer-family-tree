## Parentage & Military Service

Son of LuVerne and Shirley (Huffey) Brown. Veteran of the Vietnam War.

## Notice of Death

Richard E. Brown, 59, of Waterloo, died Friday, December 8, 2006, at his home of natural causes.

## Early Life, Marriage & Career

Richard was born on November 17, 1947, in Rock Island, Illinois, the son of LuVerne and Shirley (Huffey) Brown. He married Benita Wildebour on December 27, 1967. Richard served in the United States Navy during the Vietnam War. He worked at John Deere as an Engineering Analyst.

## Surviving Family

Richard is survived by his wife, Benita, of Waterloo; his parents, LuVerne and Shirley Brown of Waterloo; one son, Robbe (Timi) Brown of Waterloo; one daughter, Angela (Arthur) McCabe of Watertown, New York; four grandchildren; three sisters, Jackie (Herb) Clemen of Readlyn, Iowa, Janet (Bob) Hagen of Anderson, South Carolina, and Jean Downer of Kansas City, Missouri; and one brother, Lyndon Brown of Overland Park, Kansas.

## Preceded in Death

Richard was preceded in death by one son, Brian Brown; one sister, Sharon Paulson; and one brother, Ron Brown.

## Services & Memorials

Services were held at 11:00 a.m. Tuesday at Grace Lutheran Church, with burial in Garden of Memories Cemetery. Full military honors were conducted by Waterloo AMVETS Post #19 and Evansdale AMVETS Post #31. Public visitation was held from 5:00 until 8:00 p.m. Monday at Hagarty-Waychoff-Grarup Funeral Service on West Ridgeway and for one hour prior to the service at the church on Tuesday.

Memorials were directed to Cedar Valley Hospice.
