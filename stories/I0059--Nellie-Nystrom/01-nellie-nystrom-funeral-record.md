Nellie Nystrom was born January 11, 1900, and died June 18, 1971.

Her funeral service was held at Clairemont Mortuary Chapel in San Diego, California. The Rev. G. Arthur Hammons of Christ Church Unity officiated.

Nellie was interred at Mt. Hope Cemetery.

*Source: Funeral record preserved with the Steele family papers and supplied by Craig Lease.*
