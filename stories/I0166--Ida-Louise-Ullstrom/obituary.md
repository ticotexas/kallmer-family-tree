Ida Louise (Aust) Ullstrom was born on March 30, 1876, on a farm near Memphis, Nebraska, the daughter of Frank and Augusta (Lohmeyer) Aust.

On January 27, 1897, she married Andrew L. Ullstrom. The couple farmed in Saunders County for 44 years before moving to Lincoln. In January 1947, they celebrated their golden wedding anniversary.

Ida was a member of the Illiff Chapel Methodist Church, where she was active until her death.

She died at her home in Lincoln, Nebraska, on March 28, 1958, two days before her 82nd birthday.

## Family

Ida was survived by her sons, E. T. Ullstrom of Wichita, Richard R. Ullstrom of Denver, and Col. Carl L. Ullstrom of Lake Charles, Louisiana; her daughters, Crystal Wagner of Ithaca, Clara Imlay of Lincoln, and Zella Urban of Wahoo; her brother, Joe Aust; her sister, Mary McInerney; six grandchildren; seven great-grandchildren; and many nieces and nephews.

Her husband, Andrew, preceded her in death on April 19, 1949.
