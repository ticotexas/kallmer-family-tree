John Christ Urban was born in Rendsburg, Germany, on December 17, 1862.

At the age of twenty, he immigrated to the United States and settled in the state of Nebraska. During his early years there, he worked on railroad construction before purchasing land and beginning farming. He later retired from farming and moved to Ashland, Nebraska, where he spent the last seven years of his life.

## Marriage and Family

On May 4, 1890, John married Miss Anna Smidt in Omaha, Nebraska. Together they raised a family of nine children. Their eldest daughter died in infancy, while the remaining children survived him.

## Final Illness

After a lingering illness, John died at his home in Ashland on December 4, 1928, at the age of 65 years, 11 months, and 17 days.

## Faith

John was a lifelong member of the German Lutheran Church and was remembered as a faithful member throughout his life.

## Family

He was survived by his wife, Anna; four daughters, Mahla, Minna, Helen, and Ella, all of whom were living near Ashland; and four sons, Chris of Ashland, John of Winslow, Walter of Lincoln, and August of Omaha. He was also survived by three sisters and one brother, all of whom remained in Germany.

## Burial

Funeral services were held at the Congregational Church in Ashland on December 7, 1928. The Rev. Leo Duersthon officiated. Burial followed at Ashland Cemetery.
