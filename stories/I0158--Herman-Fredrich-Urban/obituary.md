Herman Friedrich Urban was born on April 23, 1893, in Chalco, Sarpy County, Nebraska, to John Christ Urban and his wife. About eight years before his death, the family moved to a farm near Ashland.

## Illness

For nearly four years Herman battled Bright's disease. During the final weeks of his illness, he was cared for at home under the constant attention of a trained nurse.

He died at the family home near Ashland, Nebraska, on December 4, 1921, at the age of 28 years, 8 months, and 11 days.

## Faith

Herman was baptized on October 15, 1893, at the Evangelical Lutheran Church in Millard, Nebraska. He was confirmed in the Evangelical Lutheran Church at Ithaca, Nebraska.

The obituary remembered him as a young man of high moral character who tried to live a life that would prepare him for his heavenly home.

## Family

He was survived by his parents, Mr. and Mrs. John Christ Urban; four sisters, Mahla, Minna, Helen, and Ella; and four brothers, Chris, John, Walter, and August. All were living at home except his brother John, who resided at Hooper.

He also left many relatives and friends who mourned his passing.

## Burial

Funeral services were held at the Congregational Church in Ashland on December 7, 1921. Following a brief prayer service at the family home, the Rev. D. M. Beggs, pastor of the church, officiated. Burial took place at Ashland Cemetery.
