Walter C. Urban was born on August 17, 1901, in Chalco, Nebraska.

On July 21, 1928, he married Zella L. Ullstrom. He spent his working life as a meat cutter before retiring and was a longtime resident of the Wahoo area.

Walter was active in his community and belonged to Pomegranate Lodge No. 110 A.F. & A.M. in Ashland, the Wahoo Eagles Lodge, and Hilltop Country Club.

He died on July 10, 1973, at the age of 71 in Wahoo, Nebraska.

Walter was survived by his wife, Zella; his son, Robert of Omaha; his sisters, Ella (Mrs. O. A. Hemmer) of Omaha and Minna Hood of Wahoo; and two granddaughters.

He was buried at Lincoln Memorial Park in Lincoln, Nebraska.
