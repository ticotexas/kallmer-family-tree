# Reverend Donald Anton Proett

## Early Life

Donald Anton Proett, son of Rev. Anton and Dena (Ferking) Proett, was born on August 14, 1910, near Rudd, Iowa. The son of a rural minister, he was the fourth of thirteen children.

Donald grew up and attended school throughout the Midwest as his father served churches in Iowa, South Dakota, and Nebraska. At an early age, he developed a fondness for farming and horses, interests he maintained throughout his lifetime.

## Education & Call to Ministry

Donald left school to begin farming but felt called to the ministry and returned to high school in 1933.

He later graduated from Hastings College and the Presbyterian Seminary in Dubuque, Iowa. He also attended Omaha Seminary and was ordained into the Presbyterian ministry on October 13, 1944, at Palmyra, Nebraska.

## Marriage

On June 5, 1938, Donald was united in marriage to Marjorie Turner at the Evangelical Church in Hastings, Nebraska, by his father, Rev. A. F. Proett.

## Ministry

The majority of Donald's life was spent serving as a rural Presbyterian minister in Nebraska.

From 1941 until 1953, he served the Presbyterian churches at Hopewell and Palmyra, Nebraska. During that time, he also taught high school in Burr, Nebraska, from 1943 to 1945.

In 1953, Reverend Proett accepted a call to serve the Czech Presbyterian and Prague Presbyterian churches. He faithfully ministered to those congregations until his retirement in 1978.

Following retirement, he remained active in ministry, supplying numerous vacant churches and officiating at weddings and funerals until shortly before his death.

## Faith

Donald received his Christian training at an early age in his family home. Throughout his adult life, he devoted himself to his ministry and to the welfare of the churches he served.

## Death

Reverend Proett passed from this earthly life on July 5, 1987, at Methodist Hospital in Omaha, Nebraska, at the age of **76 years, 10 months, and 21 days**.

He was preceded in death by his wife, his parents, three brothers, and two sisters.
