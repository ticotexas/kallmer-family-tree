Oney T. Rounds, superintendent of state highways in Susquehanna County, died at his home near Uniondale on Monday, October 21.

He had been ill with influenza and appeared to be recovering. One of his children, who was also ill, asked for a drink of water. While getting it, Oney was seized by a severe chill. His condition rapidly worsened, and he died shortly afterward.

Oney was about fifty years old. He was well known throughout the county and had many friends who mourned his untimely death.
