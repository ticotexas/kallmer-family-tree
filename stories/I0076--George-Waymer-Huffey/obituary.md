George Waymer Huffey was born on November 24, 1924, near Dalby in rural Waterville, Iowa, the son of Walter Angus and Tilda Boletta (Olson) Huffey. He was baptized and confirmed at Old East Paint Creek Lutheran Church and attended Center #7 (Dalby Country School), Decorah Elementary School, and Waterville High School, graduating in 1942. While in school, he participated in baseball and basketball.

## Farming

George was a lifelong farmer. During the Great Depression, he began farming at the age of eight alongside his grandparents, George and Inger Olson, and his uncle, Hjalmar Olson, on the family's Century Farm in Center Township.

On April 10, 1953, he married Jean Audrey Boess at Trinity Lutheran Church in Hawkeye, Iowa.

An innovative farmer, George was among the first in the area to adopt bulk milk tanks, round balers, minimum-tillage offset disks, and contour strip farming. He also sold Vigortone Premix and Crows Seed Corn, earning incentive trips to the Bahamas, Acapulco, Hawaii, Las Vegas, and San Diego.

## Faith and Community

George was a longtime member of Old East Paint Creek Lutheran Church, where he served on the church council and as president of the congregation. Later, he attended St. Paul's United Methodist Church in Waukon and Our Savior's Lutheran Church in Lansing.

## Family and Interests

George enjoyed sports throughout his life, especially watching his children and grandchildren participate in their activities. A devoted Iowa Hawkeyes fan, he attended the Rose Bowl, Peach Bowl, and Orange Bowl. He also followed the Chicago Cubs, Bears, and Bulls.

Among his many interests were bowling, dancing, horseshoes, fishing, playing cards, and preparing homemade dill pickles and horseradish for family and friends.

## Final Years

George died at his home near Waterville, Iowa, on May 17, 2016, at the age of 91.

He was survived by his wife, Jean; his children, Randall Huffey, Nancy Huffey, Dawn Huffey, Marcia Huffey, Lisa Fruechte, and Bradley Huffey; ten grandchildren; six great-grandchildren; and his sisters, Leatrice McEvilly, Esther Enyart, and Lotus Bulman.

He was preceded in death by his parents; his infant twin brothers, Thomas and Walter; his brothers, Lloyd, Vinton, and Vernon Huffey; and his sisters, Shirley Brown, Greta Lockhart, and Merle Schultz.
