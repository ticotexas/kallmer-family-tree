Gertrude (Dinges) Giers was born on October 6, 1851, in Sublette, Illinois.

About 1890, she married Alec Giers. Following their marriage, they settled on a farm in Union Township, Illinois, where they made their home for the rest of their lives.

Her husband, Alec, died in March 1930.

After an illness of several months, Gertrude died at her home west of Emington, Illinois, on June 28, 1939, at the age of 87.

She was survived by her sister, Mary Burkhardt of Sublette, Illinois, and her brother, Joseph Dinges of Odebolt, Iowa.
