## California

I was born on May 11, 1938, in Culver City, California, now part of Los Angeles. We lived for five and a half years at 2827 Alsace Avenue in Los Angeles, then two years at 2820 Mansfield Avenue, about three blocks from our first address. I attended Cienega Grade School.

My dad worked as a milk delivery man for Arden Farms Dairy in Alhambra. His route took him through Beverly Hills and the Hollywood Hills. I liked Los Angeles, but I remember being sick a lot. I also remember the air raids and the rationing of goods during World War II.

## Wahoo

In November 1945 we moved "back" to Nebraska. My folks were originally from eastern Nebraska. We stayed in Ithaca with my Uncle Fred and Aunt Crystal Wagner for about two months until my folks bought a house at 312 West 12th Street in Wahoo.

I attended West Ward Elementary School through sixth grade, North Ward for seventh and eighth grades, and then Wahoo High School, graduating in May 1956.

During high school I worked at various jobs: Ludi Printing Company, the Wahoo Locker Plant, Fairview Auto Court, Hinky Dinky, World-Herald delivery, and probably a few more.

After graduation I worked that summer, and the next four summers, for Saunders County as a surveyor's assistant. Sounds like fun, but I spent waaaay too many hours standing in the middle of gravel intersections with a pickaxe trying to find the cornerstones that had been placed there by the early mappers of Saunders County.

## College

At NU (before it was known as UNL), I took four and a half years to earn a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering, graduating on February 4, 1961.

## Western Electric

I started work at Western Electric in Omaha on February 6. I worked there for three years as a manufacturing engineer. I received an occupational deferment from the military draft because we were making wired equipment for the government (Army).

I lived with my parents until I finally got an apartment on November 1, 1963, just off 132nd and L Streets, across the road from work.

On March 1, 1964, I was transferred to our headquarters in New York to help write specifications for major projects requesting funds from the Board of Directors. I covered the Oklahoma City, Chicago, Columbus, Ohio, and Omaha plants.

I found an apartment at 555 Mount Prospect Avenue in Newark, New Jersey, and commuted to work at 195 Broadway in New York City.
