## Summer-on-Campus

In the mid-1970s I was considered for promotion to Senior Engineer several times, but management had noted that it had been a lot of years since I had been to company school. Because of home responsibilities I found excuses to avoid going away and leaving the family.

I told my boss that if they were serious, I'd like to enroll in the Summer-on-Campus program for a master's degree at Purdue. They said OK.

In 1978 I started the program. It involved five years of five weeks of classes during the summer when we had the campus nearly to ourselves.

In December 1982 I received my master's degree in Industrial Engineering by mail. Regular salary and all expenses paid made it doable. And we had a lot of fun there.

Summer-on-Campus was offered at four or five different colleges, each offering master's-level degrees.

## Senior Engineer

I was promoted to Senior Engineer in 1979. I was still in quality control, but with that ranking the boss could drop just about anything on my desk and say, "Handle it."
