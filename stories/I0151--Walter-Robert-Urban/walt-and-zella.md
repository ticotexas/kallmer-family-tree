On July 21, 1928, Zella married Walter Urban, who had also started working for Roberts Dairy as a delivery man. They stayed in Lincoln until 1933, when their adventurous spirit took them to Los Angeles. Despite it being the depth of the Depression, Walter quickly got a job delivering milk for Arden Farms Dairy.

On May 11, 1938, Zella and Walter had their only child, a son, Walter Robert. They lived in rental houses, first at 2827 Alsace Ave. and then at 2820 Mansfield Ave.

Walter was deemed too old, and now with a child, to be drafted for the huge call-up required for World War II.

In the fall of 1945, the family moved back to Nebraska, Walter being given the job of manager at the Wahoo Locker Plant. Zella kept the books there and also worked part time at the bank in Ithaca, which was run by her brother-in-law Fred Wagner. Fred was also one-third of the partnership that owned the Wahoo Locker.

Zella later worked at the Fullerton Lumber Co. in Wahoo and the First National Bank there.

Their son Bob moved to New Jersey in early 1964, and in December of that year he married Joanne Hutchins of Bloomfield, New Jersey. The new couple flew to Wahoo to spend Christmas with Walt and Zella.

In August 1965, Bob and Joanne moved back to Nebraska, Bob resuming his employment at Western Electric in Omaha.
