## Seeing the East

While in the East I managed to see some of the typical touristy stuff: the Bronx Zoo, the Staten Island Ferry for a good view of the Statue of Liberty, the Delaware Water Gap, the Empire State Building, and the 1964 World's Fair on Long Island.

I had to fly more than I liked for the job and hated waiting in airports and smelling the jet fuel. The only good thing about it was that you could get there at the last minute and get on the plane with whatever you had without being searched.

In about June 1964 I bought (ordered) a new 1964 Pontiac Catalina convertible.

## Joanne and Marriage

That fall I started dating Joanne Hutchins of Bloomfield, New Jersey, and in December (the 22nd, I believe) we were married. No, she wasn't with child.

Attending the wedding were the minister and his wife, Joanne's parents, and us newlyweds.

The apartment people wouldn't let us rent a larger unit without signing a full year's new lease, so we squeezed into my little efficiency apartment.

Joanne worked at Dr. Lewis W. Brown's allergy clinic, which was where I had met her.

## Meeting Joanne

It was September, and I was taking allergy shots at the clinic where she worked. She was the receptionist, go-fer, chief bottle washer, etc.

I watched her as people checked in, and besides being cute, what appealed to me was the way she handled the little kids coming in for their shots. Her chatty banter with them was adorable, and I thought she'd make a great mom.

I just started talking with her, and when I asked, she gave me her phone number.

We were quickly engaged. Please understand that I was totally new to the area, with no family around, and rather lonesome. Her folks liked me, and here was instant family!

She was also living at home and not getting along with her father. Everything just fell into place, and within four months we were married.

## Joanne's Family

Joanne's parents were Ken and Pauline Hutchins, both originally from eastern Pennsylvania. I understand Pauline married Ken on the rebound, and their marriage was, shall we say, an unhappy one. I understand that Ken fathered a child with another woman around the same time as Joanne, but that's all I know.

Pauline's maiden name was Haser. I met both of Joanne's grandmothers, but I believe both grandfathers were already dead.
