I know just a few details of my grandparents' lives. I know both of my grandfathers farmed at some time in their lives.

I never knew my paternal grandfather, John Christ Urban. He died before I was born.

I remember my maternal grandfather, Andrew Lewis Ullstrom. Mostly, he was all stove up with rheumatism when we'd visit my grandparents in Lincoln from 1946 to 1949, when he died. In the 1920s, he served a term in Nebraska's Legislature. I don't know what he did for work in Lincoln or what year they moved there.

As you may know, funeral handouts in those days didn't have biographies, just vital statistics and maybe a prayer.

My grandmothers, Anna Elizabeth Urban and Ida Louise Ullstrom, were in the area, but I didn't have more than a cursory relationship with them. They died within months of each other, about 1958 or 1959, I think.
