## Return to Omaha

Due to a company reorganization that enlarged the area staffs and downsized the headquarters staff, in August 1965 I was transferred back to the Omaha Works, where I was assigned as a Statistical Quality Control Engineer.

Sometime early in my career I was asked if I wanted to advance into supervision or into higher engineering responsibility. I declined the supervision route.

The company had a strong ethic for continuing engineering education, and over the years I attended classes in Chicago, New York, and locally. It probably added up to at least six or seven months altogether.

## Home and Family

We moved into an apartment near work, right off 120th and L Streets. About a year later we bought a house in Millard at 1303 East Adams.

Joanne quickly got a job at Western Electric, where she became a department secretary in Engineering. In the spring of 1967 she quit working, and Sheri was born on September 6, 1967, at the north location of Immanuel Hospital.

Sometime in 1968 we planned and contracted for the construction of the Holling Heights house. We moved in about August or September 1969. The Millard address was originally 312 Evergreen, but when Millard was annexed it became 6604 South 135th Street.

Joanne was a full-time homemaker until she started work about mid-1989. I can't remember which of her jobs came first.

Sunny, as you know, came along on April 30, 1971, at Methodist Hospital.

I won't give you a lot of detail about raising the kids. Sunny can answer any questions you may have. As I mentioned, I have detailed year-by-year summaries of what happened from 1969 through 2001. You may review them at your leisure.
