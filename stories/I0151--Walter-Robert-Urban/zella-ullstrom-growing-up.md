Zella Louise Ullstrom was born January 19, 1902, near Memphis, Nebraska, the third child of Andrew and Ida Ullstrom. Her parents farmed in the area at the time.

She attended Memphis and Ashland schools, graduating from Ashland High School in 1920. After graduation, she attended a state normal school for teacher training and then taught at a country school in the Ashland area.

While still living on the farm, she dated Walter Carl Urban, who lived and worked on a nearby farm. After a couple of years, she started work at Roberts Dairy Co. in Lincoln, where she became their cashier.
