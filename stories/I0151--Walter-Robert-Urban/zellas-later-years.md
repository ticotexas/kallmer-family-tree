Zella and Walt retired about 1967 and enjoyed trips to the Southwest, playing cards, gardening, and get-togethers with friends and relatives.

On September 6, 1967, her first grandchild, Sheri, was born in Omaha, followed by granddaughter Sunny in 1971.

On July 10, 1973, Walt passed away. He had been ill with heart disease for about a year.

Zella continued living in the house they had shared. She quit driving about 1980, and in 1983 she had a stroke serious enough to require care at a nursing home. She continued to live at Haven House in Wahoo for seven years, until she had another stroke that led to her death on March 28, 1990, at age 88.

Her funeral was at the First Methodist Church in Wahoo, where she had been a member since 1946.
