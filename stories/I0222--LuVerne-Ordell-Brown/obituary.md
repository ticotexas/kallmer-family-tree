## Notice of Death

LuVerne Ordell Brown, 91, of Tripoli, Iowa and formerly of Waterloo, Iowa, died on Friday, April 27, 2012, at the Tripoli Nursing Home, where he had been a resident for the last five years.

## Early Life & Military Service

LuVerne was born on May 2, 1920, in Choice, Minnesota, the son of Roy Ellsworth and Rose Adella (Flattum) Brown. He attended school in the Choice area. He was united in marriage to Shirley Geraldine Huffey on August 12, 1940, in Burr Oak, Iowa. He served in the United States Army in 1943.

## Career, Hobbies & Music

He worked at the John Deere Tractor Works and the Product Engineering Center in Waterloo, retiring in 1982. LuVerne and Shirley were longtime members of Grace Lutheran Church in Waterloo. LuVerne enjoyed fishing, boating, camping, traveling, woodworking, and bowling. Throughout his life he had a love for music, especially country and bluegrass. He was a self-taught guitar, harmonica, and fiddle player.

## Preceded in Death

He was preceded in death by his parents, Roy and Rose Brown; his wife, Shirley Brown; one sister, Ethel Reed; three children, Ron Brown, Sharon Paulsen, and Richard Brown; one son-in-law, Bob Hagen; two grandsons, Brian Brown and Derek Clemen; one granddaughter, Michelle Rule; two great-grandchildren, Eric Rule and Andy Harris; and two nieces, Debbie Mahoney and Cindy Norden.
