## Notice of Death

On the 23d ult., near York Springs, DENTON MYERS LEAS, son of J. E. Leas and Amanda Leas, aged 1 years, 9 months and 12 days,

## Memorial Poem

Denton little Denton has left us,<br>
To dwell with the angels above,<br>
He rests in the arms of Jesus,<br>
Who is full of mercy and love.

Father and mother, your Denton has gone,<br>
His body lies beneath the sod,<br>
His spirit has fled to that beautiful home,<br>
Where you can meet him with God.

Sisters and brothers, for him do not weep,<br>
His troubles are now at an end;<br>
He is not dead but only asleep,<br>
And ready to meet his dear friends.
