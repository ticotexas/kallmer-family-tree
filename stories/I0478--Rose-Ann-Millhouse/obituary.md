# Mrs. Rose Ann Millhouse

## Notice of Death

Mrs. Rose Ann Millhouse, 75, died at 3:00 p.m. Monday at the home of her granddaughter, Mrs. Leatha Headley, 531 West Main Street.

## Surviving Family

She is survived by three sons, Ernest, Harlan, and Earl, all of Ottumwa; two daughters, Mrs. Edna Morgan of Minneapolis, Minnesota, and Mrs. Etta Dyer of Ottumwa; twenty-five grandchildren; twenty-five great-grandchildren; and one sister, Mrs. Hilda Hopkins of Colorado.

## Services & Burial

Funeral services will be held at 1:00 p.m. Wednesday at the Johnson Funeral Chapel. The Rev. A. D. McClure, pastor of the Pentecostal Church, will officiate. Burial will be in Shaul Cemetery.
